Controlled Combustion: Building a Rocket That Behaves

Overview:

Through the University of Auckland Rocketry Club, I built and flew a Level 1 certified rocket, taking it from design through to a live launch at Orini under NZRA certification requirements. I modelled the flight profile in OpenRocket to verify stability and predict altitude, then assembled the structural, and recovery sub-systems — including motor mounting, parachute deployment, with a detachable nose cone. The rocket reached a simulated altitude of 2,400 ft and exhibited a stable flight and successful recovery, meeting the technical and safety requirements for NZRA Level 1 certification.
